Generala Rules

On your turn you have up to 3 throws with 5 dice. Tap any dice you want to keep (Hold) and roll the rest.

At the end of your turn (or after any throw), record your score in one free row of your column. Making a major hand on the first throw counts as Servida and scores bonus points:

1 to 6
Sum of all dice showing that number (e.g. three 6s = 18).
Escalera (1-2-3-4-5, 2-3-4-5-6, or 3-4-5-6-1)
20 pts — Servida: 25 pts
Full (3 of a kind + 2 of a kind)
30 pts — Servido: 35 pts
Póker (4 of a kind)
40 pts — Servido: 45 pts
Generala (5 of a kind)
50 pts — Servida: 100 pts

If your roll doesn't fit any open category, tap any free row to cross it out with 0.
